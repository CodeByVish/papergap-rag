\subsection{Preprocessing (Q4.2)}
Cleaning retains formula and citation placeholders already present in QASPER; it cannot recover missing numbers. Retrieval preprocessing is described in Section 3.1. The encoder's 512-token limit can truncate a passage without changing its stored text. Generation receives at most eight passages, each clipped to 1,200 characters, and its structured output allows limited repair of malformed escapes. A relevant passage may therefore be retrieved while its useful sentence is absent from the prompt. On the 40 development questions, 33 of the 45 mapped gold passages (73.3\%) exceed the 1{,}200-character limit and 36.6\% of their text lies beyond it; on the held-out questions 232 of the 347 mapped gold passages (66.9\%) exceed it. Section~\ref{sec:context} reports a post-hoc check with a 2{,}500-character limit; the shared code default has since been raised to that value, while the primary downstream evaluation uses 1{,}200 characters; post-hoc runs use 2{,}500, and the all-papers demonstration budget was not recorded. The same limit shows in the token counts: over 200 questions the generator received 934{,}334 prompt tokens in the static arm and 934{,}460 in the 15-passage arm, so passages beyond the eighth were retrieved but not rendered.


An earlier selected-paper demonstration on five QASPER questions gives separate evidence on context clipping. At 1{,}200 characters per passage on a laptop (Apple M5, 24 GB, Ollama \texttt{qwen3:14b}), an answerable question about evaluation metrics was refused because the metric statements began beyond the 1{,}200 characters the generator rendered, while the critic read each passage up to 3{,}000 characters. Raising the budget to 2{,}500 characters produced an answer. That refusal was a missed answer, not a successful refusal. The local runner now passes the generator's view to the critic, and the frozen evaluation runs always did; clipping itself still affects them (Q4.2). Those timings used a different model and must not be compared with \hyperref[tab:five-results]{Table~\ref*{tab:five-results}}.
